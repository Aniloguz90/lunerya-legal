Lunerya Support

Contact: support@lingoshop.app. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Readings and plans

  • The journal is free and unlimited, and stays on your device.
  • AI readings: you get 5 free readings.
  • Basic (€4.99 a month in the euro area; the price in your country is shown in the app) includes **30 readings every billing period**.
  • 5 More Readings (€2.99) adds five readings that never expire. They're used only after your free or Basic readings.

Restoring purchases

  • Basic: open Premium → Restore Purchases.
  • Purchased readings: open Premium → Restore Purchased Readings. They belong to the Apple Account that bought them; signed in with a different Apple Account, you won't see them.

Common questions

  • A reading failed. Was it counted? No. If a reading can't be created, it isn't counted against your free, Basic or purchased readings, and you can try again.
  • Do I need an internet connection? Only for AI readings. The journal works offline.
  • Can I change the language? Yes: Settings → App language, and Settings → Reading language.
  • Family Sharing: Basic and purchased readings can't be shared with Family Sharing.
  • "AI readings aren’t available in your location": our AI provider operates only in certain countries and territories. There, and while you travel through them, readings and reading purchases are unavailable. Nothing is used, your journal keeps working, and your subscription and purchased readings stay recorded. They work again where readings are available. You can still restore earlier purchases.

Cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lunerya.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

  • Your journal is stored only on your device; we don't sync or back it up. Only what you send for a reading reaches our server, and it doesn't store your dream text.
  • A dream is sent for an AI reading only when you ask, after you give permission. You can withdraw permission in Settings → Allow AI readings.
  • To delete your journal: Settings → Delete All My Dreams.
  • Full details: Privacy Policy.

Important

Lunerya offers reflective dream readings for self-exploration. It is not a medical, psychological or emergency service. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call your local emergency number (112 in Türkiye and the EU) or a crisis line right away.
